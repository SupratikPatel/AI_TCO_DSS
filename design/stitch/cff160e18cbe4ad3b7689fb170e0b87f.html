By default, macOS limits process wired memory to ~75% of physical unified memory (144 GB on 192 GB models), causing sudden Metal OOM terminations even with free RAM available. To unlock 92% headroom (up to 176 GB), apply the kernel sysctl override:

sudo sysctl iogpu.wired_limit_mb=117760
